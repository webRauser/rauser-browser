## 2026-11-23 — Sculpted Artifact Explorer

DESIGN.md §5.5 describes the Explorer's compact light and dark presentation.
The header keeps search and general controls together, and the work area groups a recessed filter rail with raised results and preview panes.
Dividers replace individual row cards, and the preview places source and summary actions next to the artifact title.
The panes can scroll independently on a roomy viewport, and a compact viewport offers a keyboard-accessible jump to the preview.
